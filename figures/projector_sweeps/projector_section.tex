\subsubsection{Projector}

In all of the following hyperparameter searches, we train with a 100\% correction policy. The reported values are Recall@1 (\%) at inference with \texttt{aggregate\_gallery\_seeks=False}. The optimal hyperparameters are not necessarily consistent across inference correction policies: runs that perform best with high correction can underperform when no correction is available, which is expected because the training distribution is biased toward fully corrected SEEK codes.
