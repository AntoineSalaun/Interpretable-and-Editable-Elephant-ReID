Subject: Which EFA dataset should we use for deployment?

Hi Kate,

I’ve checked the Elephants for Africa dataset I have—the one used for the Zooniverse experiments—to help identify exactly which collection you sent us.

The two collections are named EFA_IDI and EFA_IDU, and together contain 8,692 images:

- 5,504 identified images covering 1,008 elephant IDs.
- 3,188 unidentified images organized into 675 temporary groups. These are not necessarily 675 additional individuals; some may correspond to elephants already in the identified catalogue.

Using the original capture dates in the images’ EXIF metadata, the identified images run from 4 May 2012 to 16 June 2015, and the unidentified images from 26 September 2014 to 15 August 2015. Most identified images are from 2013–2014. There are 59 images without usable capture dates.

The images come from a mix of cameras, mainly Canon EOS 650D (2,450 images), Nikon D5100 (2,367), Canon EOS 50D (2,270), and Olympus SP570UZ (826). There are also smaller numbers from other Canon, Nikon, Panasonic, and Sony models, plus 11 Reconyx HC600 HYPERFIRE images. The camera mix differs between the identified and unidentified collections. Would this be representative of the cameras you would use for deployment?

I don’t have explicit field sighting IDs. Grouping photos within each elephant ID or temporary group, with a new encounter after a gap of more than an hour, gives approximately 2,531 individual photo encounters: 1,844 identified and 687 unidentified. That is only an estimate from timestamps. There are also very few GPS-tagged images, so it would be useful to confirm which locations this collection covers.

Now that we’re considering deployment, the plan is to replace the Zooniverse-derived SEEK codes with new predictions and expert corrections. Before investing that effort, would you prefer us to work on this collection, or would you rather send a newer or more complete dataset that better matches what you would use in practice?

If available, updated identity assignments—especially for the unidentified groups—and the original sighting/date/location records would also be very helpful. Have any of the existing IDs been merged or corrected since this version?

There is also a separate folder called EFA_S1ID with about 25,000 images, but it is not linked to the two Zooniverse manifests above. If that rings a bell, do you have a corresponding manifest or identity table for it?

Best,
Antoine
